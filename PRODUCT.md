The personal portfolio of Sakib Alam Mubin (SA Mubin). It proves elite full-stack software engineering and interactive frontend craft first, supported by security engineering awareness and teaching, while ranking cleanly for "Sakib Alam Mubin" and "SA Mubin".

- 4 pages: Home (`/`), Work (`/work/`), About (`/about/`), Contact (`/contact/`), plus `404.html`. No blog. English only.
- Interactive 3D-vinyl/clay-style developer avatar (inspired by joshwcomeau.com, custom-built for SA Mubin) used as the hero/profile centerpiece AND peeking upside-down at the footer seam with hover/click expressions.
